My Process

How every project
gets built.

From first brief to final frame — a proven, cinematic workflow designed for zero surprises and maximum impact.

Step 01

Understanding the Product / Service

Deep dive into your product, target audience, and conversion goals. Before a single frame is designed, I study your brand, competitors, and what makes your SaaS unique — so the video works strategically, not just visually.

Step 02

Script Writing

Tight, conversion-focused scripting with a strong hook and a clear CTA. Every line earns its place — no filler, no fluff. The script sets the pace and structure for everything that follows.

Step 03

Voice-Over Creation If Needed

Selecting and directing the right voice talent — tone, pace, and energy matched precisely to your brand. I brief the VO artist and ensure the delivery lands exactly right before animation begins.

Step 04

Storyboarding

A visual blueprint of the entire video — shot by shot. This is where the narrative becomes a sequence of frames, locking in pacing, camera movement, and UI flow before a single pixel is animated.

Step 05

Animation

UI-focused motion design built in After Effects and Premiere Pro. Purposeful transitions, screen callouts, zooms, and clean keyframe animation — nothing random, nothing purely decorative. Every motion has a reason.

Step 06

Music & SFX

Curating the perfect soundtrack and sound design that elevate emotion without competing with the message. Every track is licensed, mixed, and balanced for clarity across speakers, headphones, and mobile.

Step 07

Revisions

Two rounds of focused, structured feedback. I don't just execute changes blindly — if a revision hurts the video's effectiveness, I'll tell you. Every change is discussed and deliberate.

Step 08

Final Delivery

Exported in all required formats and specs — web-optimized, social cuts, captions if needed, and source files. Done means fully done. No loose ends, no back-and-forth on delivery day.
