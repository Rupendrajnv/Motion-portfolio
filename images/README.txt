HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTO
─────────────────────
1. Save your profile photo as:  profile.jpg
2. Place it in this folder:     rupendra-portfolio/images/profile.jpg
3. Upload the entire "rupendra-portfolio" folder to your hosting site.

Recommended photo specs:
  - Square or portrait crop (e.g. 600x800px)
  - File name: profile.jpg  (lowercase, exact)
  - Format: JPG or WEBP (rename to .jpg if using WEBP)

That's it — the website will automatically display your photo.
