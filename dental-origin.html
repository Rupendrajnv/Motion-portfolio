Case study · Dental Origin2026 · Freelance, remote

Paid social campaign

Dental Origin campaign.
Fifteen assets.

Dental Origin makes take-home whitening kits that dental practices sell under their own brand. The founder recorded his script on camera. I turned that footage into a full campaign: a master film, three hook versions, an offer video, five static ads and five carousels.

My role

Hook direction → Edit → Motion → Audio fix → Statics & carousels → Revisions

01 · The problemWhy practices lose the sale

Context

Patients want whiter teeth.
The practice loses the sale.

01

Patient asks

They ask about whitening during a normal visit.

02

$500 in the chair

Whitening in the dental chair costs around $500. For most patients, that's too much.

03

Sale walks out

They buy a generic kit online or at a big store instead.

What Dental Origin sells

Take-home whitening kits, branded with each practice's own logo, colours and packaging. Patients get an affordable option from the dentist they already trust.

The campaign's job

Paid social ads that get practice owners to book a call with Dental Origin. The audience is dentists, not patients.

02 · The hookThe first 3 seconds

The decision

Lead with the dentist,
not the founder.

Before · How the master film opens

“Hey, you've probably seen our custom branded teeth whitening kits all over your feed. My name is…”

It starts with the speaker. That works for people who already know the brand, but a cold ad needs to name the viewer's problem first.

After · Three openings for the short ads

Hook 1 · Fear of loss

“Dentists, avoid this mistake when selling teeth whitening.”

Hook 2 · Curiosity

“Dentist. The real reason you're not selling teeth whitening is this.”

Hook 3 · Call-out

“If you're a dentist struggling to sell teeth whitening, listen carefully.”

The same body after every hook · about 50 seconds

Demand is high→$500 feels too much→Custom kits from their own dentist→40,000+ patients→Book a call

Only the first line changes. So when the ads run, the results show which opening works, and the rest of the edit stays the same.

The founder's call

“I think starting with me has more trust.”

Eric, founder · client feedback, 26 March 2026

So the master film kept his opening, and the three short versions open with the dentist's problem.

04 · The master film16:9 · 69 seconds

Craft

A founder story,
edited to keep moving.

The founder filmed himself talking. I cut it to 69 seconds, removed fillers and repeated words, and built the visuals around his words: captions that move with his voice, product shots, simple charts and a clear call to action. Something new appears every few seconds, so a busy dentist keeps watching.

Some kit images and store-shopping scenes were made with Nano Banana and Google Flow, then matched to Dental Origin's colours.

05 · FeedbackWhat changed in revisions

What went wrong

“Too distracting.”
So I cut the 3D.

What I made

3D objects between scenes.

In my first version, 3D objects flew across the screen as transitions. I liked how they looked.

His feedback

They pulled focus.

The founder said they took attention away from what he was saying.

The fix

Clean and simple.

I removed them and kept simple cuts and moves, so the message lands first.

Another fix · Audio

A broken section, fixed without a reshoot.

A microphone glitch made one recorded section unusable. I recreated the founder's voice with ElevenLabs and patched just that section, so it blends into the rest of his recording.

The lesson

Motion should carry the message, not compete with it.

06 · New angle9:16 · 76 seconds

Re-cut

A new offer,
with no new filming.

From the same footage, I cut a second pitch. Instead of “here is a kit”, it sells Dental Origin as a full system for the practice.

01

Your brand on the box

Kits designed and made with the practice's own logo and colours.

02

Position it right

Whitening as a way to attract patients, not a discount.

03

14-day staff launch

A simple plan so the team offers it, not just the dentist.

04

Ongoing support

Assets and help to keep it running.

Claims in this video, like patient numbers, are the client's own.

07 · The resultHonest numbers

Result

Live on their site,
and they came back for more.

What I can show

My master film is the hero video on Dental Origin's whitening page.

The founder shared a cropped ads screenshot showing revenue from the ads, and asked me for more work.

“Video is really good.”

Eric, founder · 26 March 2026

What I can't

I had no access to the ad account. I don't know which hook won, and I have no numbers I can share.

The lesson

Next time, agree how we'll measure success before launch, and ask for access to the results.
