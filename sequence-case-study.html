Case Study SaaS Explainer · Fintech Concept Project

Turning a finance bottleneck into finance without friction.

A self-initiated explainer for Sequence, an AI-native platform that automates billing, invoicing and revenue recognition for finance teams. I wrote the script, built the storyboard, designed every frame in Figma and animated it in After Effects. Solo, in 9 days.

My roleScript · Storyboard · Design · Animation

ToolsFigma · After Effects

Concept project. Not commissioned by or affiliated with Sequence. All figures shown in the film are illustrative.

01 The challenge

A deep product. A one-minute window.

Sequence does a lot: quotes, contracts, usage-based billing, invoicing, collections, revenue recognition, and AI agents that handle the follow-ups. But finance leaders don't buy a feature list. They buy the end of month-end chaos.

So the job was never to show every feature. It was to make one feeling land in under a minute: your billing can run itself, and you can trust it.

02 The hook

“Your finance team isn’t slow.
Your system is.”

0:02
0:03

Those are the first eight words of the voiceover. Most finance software ads open on the product. I opened on blame, and then moved it.

Finance teams hear “why is this taking so long?” all the time. The first two seconds agree with that feeling, then flip it. The problem isn't the people, it's the system. The viewer goes from defensive to nodding in about three seconds, and the story now has a villain that Sequence can replace.

03 The script

106 words. Every sentence earns its place.

The whole film hangs on this voiceover. Plain finance words, one idea per sentence, and Sequence isn't named until the cost of the problem is clear. The colors match the five acts of the film.

Your finance team isn’t slow. Your system is. Every deal triggers a chain of manual work: contracts, billing, revenue tracking, all across disconnected tools. That’s how revenue gets delayed, errors happen, and finance becomes a bottleneck. Sequence replaces that entire workflow with one intelligent system. Just send a contract. Sequence reads it, understands the terms, and automatically handles billing, invoicing, and revenue recognition. Its AI agents don’t just track data; they take action, following up, updating records, and keeping everything accurate in real time. Fully compliant, audit-ready, and built for scale. So your team can stop fixing spreadsheets and start scaling revenue. Sequence: finance without friction.

05 Art direction

Two visual worlds, one switch

The problem and the solution don't share a single visual rule. Problem scenes are red on deep navy, crowded, with alerts stacking on top of each other. Solution scenes are calm blue light with room to breathe.

You feel which side of the story you're on before you read a word.

06 Scene by scene

Every scene has one job

If a shot couldn't say why it was there, it didn't make the cut. Each beat below shows the voiceover line, what's on screen, and why it's there.

0:04

The noise

VO“Every deal triggers a chain of manual work…”

A laptop drowning in failed Stripe payments, Slack pings and “start closing deals” emails.

Why it's thereEvery finance lead has seen this screen at 11pm on day 30. Recognition earns attention faster than any claim.

0:07

One deal, five systems

VO“…contracts, billing, revenue tracking, all across disconnected tools.”

A $240k contract has to travel through QuickBooks, billing, contracts, invoices and revenue. Then the connections snap.

Why it's thereThis is the invisible part of the problem. Drawing the journey shows why things break without a single word of explanation.

0:13

Put a price on it

VO“That’s how revenue gets delayed, errors happen…”

23 days of delay. $4.2M in invoicing errors. One number per card, one card per beat.

Why it's thereCFOs think in days and dollars. Pain without a number is just a mood.

0:16

Land the problem

VO“…and finance becomes a bottleneck.”

“Finance becomes bottleneck,” big and red. The last dark frame before the switch.

Why it's thereThe whole first act builds to this line. It's the lowest point, so the switch that follows feels like relief.

0:19

The switch

VO“Sequence replaces that entire workflow with one intelligent system.”

Hard cut from dark to white. The Sequence name, then “One intelligent” builds on screen.

Why it's thereThe brand reveal sits at the biggest contrast moment in the film, so it's the frame people remember.

Start where it broke

VO“Just send a contract.”

A signed service agreement drops into frame.

Why it's thereAct one broke at the contract, so act two starts with the contract. The solution answers the exact problem we just watched.

ASC 606, GDPR, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 badges stack up under the line.

Why it's thereNo finance team switches billing systems without this. Skipping it would quietly kill the deal.

0:50

Customer cards and a revenue dashboard for the payoff, then the tagline and one URL on a clean end card.

Why it's thereThe tagline is the exact opposite of the opening problem, so the story ends where it began, solved.

07 Craft decisions

Small decisions that make it feel premium

01

Serif for emotion, sans for data

Story lines use a high-contrast italic serif, so they feel human. UI labels and numbers stay in a clean sans, so data reads instantly.

02

UI rebuilt, never recorded

Every card was redesigned in Figma with only what the scene needs: one number, one label. Real product screens are too dense at this speed.

03

Motion that matches the mood

Problem scenes shake, stack and collide. Solution scenes glide in on soft easing with longer holds. The pacing tells the story too.

04

Depth only where it counts

The 3D glass blocks appear only in the action beat, so the moment the agent starts working feels heavier than everything around it.

05

Say it, then show it

The key word of each line lands on screen as type, right as it's spoken: slow, system, bottleneck, read, understand, real time. The story still reads with the sound off.

06

Paced for a finance buyer

106 words in 51 seconds is about 125 words a minute, calmer than a typical ad read. Every claim gets a beat to land before the next one arrives.
